# Foreword

This book began on a winter night when the power failed and the candles came out, and I realized that stories are how we keep ourselves warm.

# Chapter One

The harbor was empty when she arrived, the water flat as hammered tin under a low grey sky that promised more snow before nightfall.

She walked the length of the pier twice, counting the bollards, before she let herself look up at the lighthouse.

* * *

By dusk the lamp was lit, and the keeper's daughter watched the beam sweep the dark water like a slow and patient hand.
